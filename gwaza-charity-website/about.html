OUR STORY

Practical, community-led support for a brighter future.

GWAZA Charity Foundation Uganda Limited is a registered charitable organization committed to improving the lives of children, families and vulnerable people. We work with communities in Eastern Uganda, responding to local needs through support for education, healthcare and stronger livelihoods.

We believe meaningful change grows when people work together. By connecting care with opportunity, we aim to help individuals and families build healthier, more hopeful futures.
